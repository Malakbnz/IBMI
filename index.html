01 THE TEAM BEHIND THE IDEAS

HELLO, WE’RE IBMI.

Big ideas.
Brave builds.

IBMI #12141 is a FIRST Robotics Competition team from Morocco, united by a shared curiosity for engineering and a belief in what we can create together.

We bring different perspectives to one workbench: designing, experimenting, solving problems and learning from every iteration. Our team is where imagination meets practical skills — and where collaboration turns ambitious ideas into something real.
